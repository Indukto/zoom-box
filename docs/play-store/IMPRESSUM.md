## DSA trader information

- Play Console → Monetisation setup → **EU Digital Services Act**: declare
  trader status and enter the **same** name/address/email/phone as above.
  Google verifies (bank or business document) and displays it on the
  listing; Art. 30 DSA.
- If you distribute **paid** or ads-funded apps, or apps in significant
  volume, you are a trader under Art. 2 (4) DSA in practically all cases.
- If you are a **non-EU resident**: the DSA trader declaration is made to
  Google regardless of your residence; additionally consider appointing an
  EU responsible person (GPSR, see checklist) and naming them here.
